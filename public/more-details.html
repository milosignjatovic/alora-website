01 / 06

Log your day

A few taps, any time of day. Everything starts from the A button in the middle of the tab bar.

01Home tab

Your day at a glance

Home opens on today: the date strip, your Alora Score with how the day compares to your usual, your plan for the day and everything you logged, in order.

  • Tap a day in the strip to look back.
  • The score ring tells you how many signals it already has.
  • Every entry on the timeline opens for a quick edit.

02Home → the A button

Log anything in two taps

The A button opens the launcher. The two quick buttons at the top count a stool or a glass of water in one tap; the tiles below open a full entry.

  • Stool, symptom, meal, medicine, activity, water and notes.
  • Urgent trips, subocclusion episodes, labs and procedures.
  • The card and the meal scan are one tap away, too.

Tip The badges show what you logged today, so you never count twice.

03Launcher → Stool

A stool entry, without the awkwardness

Pick the Bristol type from pictures, then urgency and blood. Seven more details wait behind "Show 7 more" for the days you want them.

  • Bristol types read with their full description.
  • Time defaults to now and is easy to change.
  • Alora uses the entry for your score and patterns.

04Launcher → Symptom

Name what you feel

Tap every symptom that fits, from cramping to joint pain, set how strong it is and add a note if you want to remember something.

  • Symptoms around the anus have their own tags.
  • One entry can carry several symptoms.
  • A note stays with the entry and goes to your report.

05Launcher → Meal

Meals, by photo or by hand

Choose the meal type, then scan your plate or add the dishes yourself. After a few weeks Alora links what you ate to the days after.

  • Scan a meal and let the camera read your plate.
  • Pick from a food catalog made for your country.
  • A few words of description are enough on a busy day.

06Launcher → Meal → Scan a meal

Photograph your plate, get the ingredients

Take a photo of your meal and Alora names what is on the plate. Check the list, switch off anything that is not right, and add it all to your meal with one tap.

  • Each ingredient can be changed or switched off before you save.
  • Add up to five photos for a bigger meal.
  • The photo goes to an AI service to be read; Alora does not keep it.

Tip The plate shown here is an illustration. The ingredient list is what the app read from it.

07Launcher → Check-in

The daily check-in

Once a day, say how you are: well-being, pain, fatigue, fever, sleep. Alora prefills it from your typical values, so most days are a quick confirm.

  • Sliders for pain, fatigue and sleep.
  • Prefilled from how a usual day feels for you.
  • It feeds the clinical part of your Alora Score.

08Launcher → Lab

Lab results, read from a photo

Photograph your lab report, or pick a photo or a PDF, and Alora fills the values into the right fields. It happens on your phone. You check them and save.

  • Every value Alora read is marked, so you know what to check.
  • Blood count, biochemistry, stool and other panels.
  • The report stays attached to the entry.

Tip The report shown here is a sample made for this tour.

02 / 06

Understand your days

Alora reads your log and shows what goes with your better days and your harder ones.

09Insights tab

What Alora noticed

Alora looks through your entries for patterns worth knowing and shows each one as a card, with how often it happened and what came with it.

  • Each card shows the days behind the pattern.
  • Related patterns fold under the main one.
  • Some cards suggest mentioning it at your next appointment.

Tip Alora describes what your log shows. It never diagnoses.

10Insights → Analyze

Your days, side by side

Analyze sets your recent days against your usual pattern: how many were closer, in line or further from it, how they felt, and how your Alora Score moved.

  • Ranges from one week to six months.
  • Your usual pattern drawn next to your score.
  • Constipation and other episodes marked on the chart.

11Insights → Analyze, further down

Lab results over time

Calprotectin, CRP, ferritin and vitamin D on one timeline, with the healthy range shaded and your biologic doses marked, so you can see what changed after a dose.

  • Values come from a photo of your lab report.
  • In range, borderline and high bands at a glance.
  • One reading tells you when to log another.

12Insights → Explore

New research, picked for you

Every day Alora picks articles about living with IBD from trusted sources, from guidelines to lifestyle, so you can keep up and bring questions to your doctor.

  • Filter by guidelines, ingredients or lifestyle.
  • Official sources are marked.
  • Each article links to its source.
03 / 06

Your health, in one place

Diagnoses, history, therapy and medicine supply, kept together and ready for your doctor.

13Health tab

Everything about your condition

The Health tab holds what does not change every day: your diagnosis, medical history, life stage, typical values, therapy and documents.

  • Each row tells you what is already recorded.
  • Demo plans are marked, so you know what to replace.
  • The can't-wait card lives here too.

14Health → Medical history

Your medical history

Allergies, past treatments, surgeries, hospital stays, other conditions and complications, each with its own add button. All of it goes into your doctor report.

  • Past treatments keep the dates and why they stopped.
  • Complications record location, status and treatment.
  • Risk factors such as smoking have their own card.

15Health → Therapy and schedule

Your therapy and schedule

Medicines, activities, labs and procedures on one schedule, today first. Alora reminds you, and keeps track of what is coming up.

  • Filter by medicines, labs or activities.
  • Each plan shows its time and how often it repeats.
  • Stock counts tell you how long a supply lasts.

16Health → PillPal

Medicine that doesn't run out

PillPal counts what you have of each medicine and how long it will last, so you can refill before you run out.

  • Days left for each medicine.
  • Plans without stock are listed apart.
  • Add a medicine in one step.
04 / 06

Before your appointment

Everything your doctor should see, on a few pages you can send or show.

17Health → My Documents

Your documents

Your doctor reports and anything you add by camera, from your photos or as a file, in one list you can share from.

  • Add a scan, a photo or a PDF.
  • Share any document from its row.
  • Reports keep the period they cover.

18My Documents → a report

A report for your doctor

A few pages with what matters since your last visit: your Alora Score, bowel movements, days with symptoms, therapy adherence, medical history and lab trends.

  • Send it as a PDF, or show it on your phone.
  • A link you share is encrypted on your phone.
  • The link stops working when you say so.
05 / 06

Out and about

When you can't wait, Alora speaks for you and finds the nearest toilet.

19Launcher → Card

The can't-wait card

A card that asks to use a toilet, so you don't have to explain. Show it full screen, in the language of the country you are in.

  • Works offline.
  • Choose the card's language.
  • Show your diagnosis on it only if you want to.

Tip Tap "Log this trip" afterwards to keep the urgent trip in your log.

20Card → Find a toilet

Toilets nearby

The nearest toilets around you, with what each one offers: free, accessible, customers only, changing table.

  • Sorted by distance.
  • Missing a toilet? Add it.
  • Open any of them in your maps app.
06 / 06

Make it yours

Language, look, notifications and your account.

21Settings tab

Settings, your way

Your account, language, appearance, notifications and integrations. Demo data can be removed in one step when you start logging your own.

  • Ten languages, Serbian Latin and Cyrillic included.
  • Light, dark or automatic appearance.
  • Choose which gentle nudges Alora sends.

Tip Alora follows your phone's larger text setting and works with VoiceOver.

That's Alora

Coming soon to iPhone and Android. Until then, keep this tour: save it as a PDF and share it with whoever helps you with your condition.

The screens show demo data. Alora doesn't diagnose: anything it notices is worth checking with your doctor.
