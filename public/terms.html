What Alora is

Alora is a companion app for people living with IBD, IBS and other gut conditions. It helps you record your days, see patterns, and prepare for appointments.

Not medical advice

Alora doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't replace your doctor. Anything it notices is worth checking with your doctor. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
