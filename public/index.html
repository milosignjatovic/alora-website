Made for the days that are hard to explain

Three things Alora does every day, so you don't have to keep it all in your head.

01

Snap your meal

Take a photo and Alora recognises the food on your plate. After a few weeks it links your meals to the days after them.

02

Spot your patterns

Alora shows you what goes with your better days, and what with your harder ones.

03

Be ready for your appointment

Alora picks out what matters from your entries and prepares questions for your doctor, on one page.

Everything Alora can do

From a quick log on the go to the report you bring to your visit.

Your day in one number

The Alora Score sums up your day from 0 to 100. Alora follows it, your digestion and anything that stands out, and tells you when a day is further from your usual rhythm.

A report for your doctor

What stands out since your last visit, how consistently you took your therapy, and the symptoms worth mentioning. Send it as a PDF, or show it on your phone.

Lab results, read for you

Photograph a lab report and Alora reads the values out of it, so your biomarkers sit next to the rest of your days, with your therapy marked on the chart.

Medicine that doesn't run out

Plan your therapy with reminders and notes like "take after a meal". Alora keeps count of your supply and tells you when it is time to refill.

Your medical history, in one place

Allergies, past treatments, surgeries, hospital stays, complications and risk factors, kept together and added to your doctor report.

Episodes, logged when they happen

Log a subocclusion episode with how long it lasted and what came with it. Alora keeps the episodes together and suggests when to mention them to your care team.

Here when you can't wait

A card to show someone without having to explain, in the language of the country you are in, and the nearest toilets. The card works offline.

Easy to read, for everyone

Larger text that wraps instead of clipping, VoiceOver labels on every control, light and dark themes, and ten languages.

Privacy first

Your health log is yours

Living with a gut condition is personal. Alora is built so that what you record about your health stays with you.

  • What you log about your health stays on your phone and never goes to AI.
  • A report you share by link is encrypted on your phone. Alora can't open it, and the link stops working when you say so.
  • You decide what to share, and with whom.

Alora doesn't diagnose. Anything it notices is worth checking with your doctor.
